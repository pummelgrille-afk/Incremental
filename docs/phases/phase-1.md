## Decisions locked

- **Setting: The Orrery.** A clockwork machine that drives the real heavens.
- **Objective: the Mainspring**, health stat **Tension**.
- **Allies: Movements** (ring slots) · **Support: Chimes** (static rim mounts).
- **Enemy: the Unwinding**, creatures called **Slack**. Entropy, not a faction.
- **Prestige: the Rewinding.**
- **Core input: the ring nudge** — the single live combat input, resolving the
  tension between "auto-battle" and "bullet-hell dodging" in the plan.

## Notes

Setting chosen by the user from a shortlist of four. The Orrery won on mechanical
grounds: the formation surface is already in motion, spiral patterns are native to
the fiction, conjunction is a synergy mechanic unique to a rotating board, and
prestige-as-rewinding is diegetic.
